I. THE LEGEND

In the annals of history, few names shine as brightly as Alexander of Macedon — the boy who tamed an untamable horse, unmade the Gordian Knot, and wore the crowns of Greece, Egypt, and Persia before he was thirty.

But the old chronicles got one thing wrong: they thought the story ended in Babylon. It did not. It merely waited — two thousand three hundred years — for the heir to arrive.

Lucas Alexander Barrera. Same fire in the eyes. Same refusal to accept that anything is impossible. The chroniclers have been summoned. The campaign has already begun.

«οὐδὲν ἀνυπόστατον τοῖς θαρροῦσιν»— “Nothing is impossible for the brave.”
